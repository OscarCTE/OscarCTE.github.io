San Francisco Crime 2003–2025

This project explores long-term crime patterns across San Francisco using public datasets and interactive visualizations. The goal is to uncover trends across districts, time, and geography to better understand urban dynamics.

Crime by Police District

Crime varies significantly across San Francisco’s police districts. This visualization highlights how different areas evolve over time, revealing both persistent hotspots and changing trends.

Hourly Crime Distribution

Crime is not evenly distributed throughout the day. This interactive chart reveals peak activity periods and quieter hours, helping identify temporal patterns.

Crime Density Heatmap

The heatmap visualizes spatial crime density across the city, highlighting clusters of high activity and areas with relatively low crime.
